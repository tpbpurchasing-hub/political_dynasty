What is Political Dynasties?

A political dynasty is a situation where members of the same family hold or seek political positions,
either at the same time or across different periods.

How does Political Dynaties Work?

Political families may have several relatives who serve as elected officials.
One family member may hold a position while another relative runs for a different position or succeeds them later.
